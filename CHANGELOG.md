# Changelog

All notable changes to M-GBA are documented here.

## Unreleased

### Added

- Data registry schema `mgba-data-registry-0.2` (ADR-0021 amendment 1, 2026-09-15): every entry now carries machine-readable `provenance.license_ids` (controlled SPDX / `LicenseRef-*` vocabulary) and `provenance.source_refs` (`dataset:` / `doi:` / `geo:` / `url:` / `repo:`). `mgba data-check` additionally enforces the vocabulary, ref formats, that external data cites an external source, that `dataset:` refs resolve, that downstream entries keep every upstream licence id (licence propagation), and that `repo:` refs exist; its output lists `license_ids` per entry. All 26 entries migrated and pass; prose fields are unchanged. `mgba evidence-report` accepts registry schema 0.1 and 0.2.

- Registry entries accept an optional `distribution` (`repository` default, or `local-only`; ADR-0021 amendment 2). A local-only file is still SHA-256 checked when present; when absent, `data-check` reports it explicitly instead of failing. The local-only set is pinned by a test to the two GEO GSE264667 bundles, whose recorded licence forbids redistribution.

### Scientific boundary

- ADR-0022 display projections remain observation-only. ADR-0023 adds a separate causal spatial L3, but its unit-square coordinates, grid, movement, mixing, and source/sink coefficients are dimensionless and unfitted: it is not a physical diffusion solver, spatial-omics reconstruction, or biological/clinical predictor. The fixed-graph CAR-M model remains the permanent control.
- The first spatial run's favorable directed L4 proxy deltas do not establish efficacy or realism: the endpoint class is unchanged, L3 mechanisms are not yet internally ablated, no spatial ground truth is used, and the identical deterministic seed repeats do not support inferential statistics.
- ADR-0024 main effects are deterministic rule-family contrasts, not biological effect sizes. The topology and field directions interact, and the audit deliberately uses one replay seed instead of presenting duplicate deterministic runs as statistical evidence.
- Feedback is a downward constraint channel only: it reshapes L3 resource ceiling / stress floor at the L4 slow clock, never prescribes L2 actions, and every constraint enters the L3 source-digest hash chain for auditability.
- E4 reference conditions currently end `FAILED` in all synthetic regimes under the unfitted reference parameters; these are retained as negative baselines, not tuned for display.
- The active-inference policy is a reference-model experiment, not a claim that free-energy minimization is biologically validated; under unfitted parameters all policy conditions still end `FAILED` in every synthetic regime, retained as negative baselines.
- Charts show dimensionless reference-model proxies; L0–L3 series are per-tick means across cells, and L4 is charted only at scheduled slow-clock observations.
- Dynamic topology is an E5 reference comparison, not a validated self-organization result: under frozen governance thresholds the default scenario commits zero swaps and all synthetic regimes still end `FAILED`; negative baselines retained without tuning.

- E3 grading implements — and deliberately stops short of — validated results: an `emergence_candidate` grade under unfitted parameters means the pre-registered criteria were met, not that emergence is proven; frozen-protocol statistics and layer ablations remain future work per AGENTS.md §4.
- Stability grading is a reference-model diagnostic only: under unfitted controller parameters, all closed-loop conditions currently grade `deterioration`/`lock`/`indeterminate` with no `recovery`, an honest negative baseline for the E4 feedback question.
- The neural layer variants are frozen-weight contract demonstrators, not trained models: the repository holds no supervision targets or training data, so the MLP/GNN weights are derived deterministically from explicit seeds and quantify only the trajectory impact of a layer swap — no neural-fidelity, training-capability, or compute-savings claims are made.
- L2 calibration is a pre-registered reference-model diagnostic, not display tuning: the first run (search digest `99ce6e355b8e0afa…`) lifted train `mean_homeostasis` 0.2076→0.5927 and improved all four held-out stress regimes (verdict `generalized`; calm/injury flip `failed → stable`, severe/collapse remain `failed`). Several calibrated coordinates sit on the [0,1] box edges, so the in-class optimum may lie outside the declared box; widening it requires a new pre-registered protocol. No biological or clinical fitting is claimed, and the calibrated config does not enter any frozen experiment protocol.
- The latent-cell policy is a frozen-random-weight contract demonstrator of the AI-virtual-cell architecture class, not a trained virtual cell: on the default scenario its cross-layer effects are ≤1e-12 with identical (`FAILED`) outcomes, and all 24 synthetic-benchmark runs end `FAILED` — retained as a null/negative baseline without tuning. No virtual-cell realism or superiority claims are made; coupling real AIVC datasets or pretrained models to L0/L1 is future work requiring its own download authorization and ADR.
- The autolab scheduler is a methodology tool, not an autonomous-discovery claim: its hypothesis space is closed and pre-registered, the selection policy and effect scalar are frozen before any run, and every conclusion stays at reference-model level. Self-confirmation (hypotheses validated on the model that generated them) and metric gaming are mitigated by frozen protocols, held-out synthetic regimes, and the future real-data anchoring in `data/aivc/`; the LLM proposal channel is gated and never self-executing.
- The OCellus-derived additions adopt modular governance and representation contracts only. ClockSector has no real-target superiority result, and the perturbation anchor has only synthetic contract fixtures because OCellus code/weights/license and an authorized empirical split are not locally available. Pseudo-bulk differential-expression predictions are not single-cell temporal dynamics; natural-language explanations are not treated as faithful GNN mechanisms.
- The co-culture scenario is an abstract reference-level convention proof (ADR-0021): Genome templates named "signaling"/"repair" do not claim real cell-type identity, and under the demo stress load both populations reach the DEAD absorbing state by tick 4 — consistent with the frozen 100-tick `FAILED` negative baseline, retained without tuning.
